Views by format

What the winners have in common

  • Split all 12 signs. The biggest reels (40.9M, 25.4M, 6.9M) put six signs on one side and six on the other, so every viewer is included and sends it to someone on "the other side".
  • One big text box on frame 1. A short question or label in a solid box ("Two types of wives", "Who will you marry?"). That frame is also the thumbnail.
  • A share or comment trigger, never a sales line. "Send this to your bestie", "Choose your birth month", "Comment to get your sketch". Comment-bait reels hit 27K to 71K comments.
  • Short and silent-friendly. Most winners run about 7 to 15 seconds and read fine with the sound off.
  • Repeat what worked. The 898K sketch hook was posted again and pulled 253K. Top accounts reuse their best template every week.
  • Faceless works. AI cinematic images with bold yellow and white captions (363K, 306K) and moving b-roll with a text box (170K) need no presenter.
