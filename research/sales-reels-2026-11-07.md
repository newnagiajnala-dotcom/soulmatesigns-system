## What makes them sell
1. Short: 5–13 s for the info/affirmation formats → replays. Reaction format 16–22 s.
2. Interaction in the video: affirm YES · save this · pick 1/2/3 · screenshot · find your letter · comment a word. That drives comments/saves → reach.
3. The SELL is in the caption + bio, not the video: "DRAW your soulmate sketch today 👇 Must give it a try, link in bio".
4. Comment → DM: on the big reels most comments are "Link" / "🔗" → the creator DMs the link (manual or automation). Comments also boost reach.
5. Volume: winning accounts have 1,000–6,000 posts. Many reels, same few formats.
6. Bio = one direct promise + link ("Want to meet your soulmate? Click here now 👇").
