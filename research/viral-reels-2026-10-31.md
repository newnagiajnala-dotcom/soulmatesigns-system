# Viral reel research — soulmate / zodiac niche (31 Oct 2026)

Method: Instagram keyword searches (soulmate signs, soulmate sketch, future spouse, zodiac love,
twin flame signs, who is my soulmate) → identified accounts → scanned their Reels grids for play counts
→ read captions of top performers.

Scanned: ~257 reels from 7 account grids + ~110 posts from keyword searches (likes/comments only).

Honest finding: true 1M+ reels in the core soulmate/zodiac niche are rare. Only 1 reel over 1M in the
sample (3.3M, a general "comment for link" tech account). Typical viral ceiling in-niche: 200K–900K.
