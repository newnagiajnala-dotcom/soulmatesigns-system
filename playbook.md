# Playbook — @soulmatesigns.daily

The system reads this before making every reel and updates it after reading stats.
Start state = research from 31 Oct 2026 (research/viral-reels-2026-10-31.md).

## Rules (never break)
- Honest framing: curiosity/entertainment. Never "guaranteed real soulmate", no fake testimonials,
  no fake "I tried it and it worked" stories, no income/health claims.
- Never repost or copy another creator's video, audio voice or exact script. Copy FORMATS only.
- Every reel ends with one CTA. Bio link = bridge page → hoplink (affiliate=abhi10k).
- Disclose affiliate link on the bridge page.

## Hooks that win (first 1–2 seconds, on-screen text)
- "Pick a pile — this is your future spouse"
- "Find your birth month — someone is thinking of you"
- "3 signs you should marry the moment you meet them"
- "If this reached you, don't skip"
- "Comment your sign — I'll tell you who your soulmate is"
- "Which sign makes the best partner? Wrong answers only"

## Production defaults (text reels, faceless)
- 9:16, 7–15 s, big readable text, 2–4 text beats, dreamy night/pastel backgrounds.
- Beat 1 = hook (≤ 8 words). Last beat = CTA.
- CTA rotation: "Comment your sign 👇" / "Save this" / "Tag them" / "Link in bio for your sketch 💫".
- Caption: 1-line hook + 3–5 niche hashtags (#soulmate #zodiac #twinflame #astrology #lovesigns).

## Funnel logic
- F0 memes = reach + followers (no selling). F1/F2/F4 = clicks to bio link (selling).
- F0 must be ORIGINAL scenes/text (no reused clips) — e.g. text-message memes, "signs as texts", chat screenshots, illustrated scenes.

## Mix per week (14 reels)
- 4× F0 zodiac label memes, 3× F1 pick-a-pile, 2× F2 birth month, 2× F3 zodiac, 2× F4 comment-bait, 1× F5.
- Every 7 days: double the format with best views/reel, drop the worst.

## Audience: Tier-1 (added 1 Nov 2026)
- Target viewers: US, UK, Canada, Australia — mostly women 18–34. Optimise every hook for them.
- Language: natural US English, Gen Z/millennial tone, US spelling. Situations: texting, dating apps, situationships,
  ghosting, roommates, coffee dates, work/college life. No India-specific references, currency or Hinglish.
- Posting times (US Eastern): 7:41 AM (UK lunchtime + US morning) and 7:41 PM (US evening peak).
- Signals that matter most for Tier-1 reach: shares (send to bestie) and saves — every CTA asks for one of them.

## Music
- Every reel has original background music baked in (reelgen/music.py): playful / dreamy / emotional by format.
- Trending Instagram library songs can't be added via API and copyrighted songs get muted, so we never use them.
- Learning: from week 2, compare views by music mood and keep the best one per format.
